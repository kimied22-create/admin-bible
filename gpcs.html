Process

GPC Flow

1. Contact & Schedule

Review the appeal request, log the student, contact the parent using their preferred method, and document the three communication attempts when needed.

2. Prepare

Gather academic data, outreach history, and the student summary/decision form before entering the meeting.

3. Facilitate

Hold the formal committee meeting in Google Meet with the required committee members and guide the conversation using the script.

4. Notify & Archive

Complete the decision documents, send the parent notification within 24-48 hours, upload the PDF, and link final records in the tracking sheet.
